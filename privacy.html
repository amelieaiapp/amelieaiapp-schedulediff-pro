ScheduleDiff Pro XER XML — Privacy

ScheduleDiff Pro runs entirely on your device. Programme files you open are read locally in the browser. They are not uploaded to AMELIE.AI or any server.

Optional history (batch ids, file names, hashes, change counts) may be stored in your browser’s local storage on this device only. You can clear it from the app or by clearing site data.

No account is required. No analytics or tracking are built into the app.
